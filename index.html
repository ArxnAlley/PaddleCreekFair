The heart behind Paddle Creek Paints

Every piece you see is painted by hand in my home studio in Catlettsburg, Kentucky. I started Paddle Creek Paints with a simple goal — to create art that brings warmth, beauty and meaning into your everyday life.

I don't work in one style. A horse portrait in gold leaf, ghosts on a cutting board, a senior's name down the leg of a pair of jeans. They all come off the same table. What stays the same is that it is my hands, and one piece at a time.
